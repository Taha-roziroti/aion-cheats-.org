# Aion Classic ESP and aimbot pricing: monthly vs lifetime

**Aion Classic ESP aimbot pricing** is the first thing most buyers compare before checkout.

Before you buy the main plan, the usual question is total cost.

The published listing offers two access options for **ESP and aimbot** on the main plan. Monthly access costs $35 for 30 days. Lifetime access costs $150 as a one-time payment.

Both plans include the same listed ESP and aimbot modules.

Cloud DMA and HWID Spoofer are **not** included in those prices. They are separate supporting products with their own checkout links.

## Monthly plan ($35 / 30 days)

The monthly plan is the lower upfront option.

The main plan includes the partner-listed **ESP** and **aimbot** modules only—not Cloud DMA or HWID Spoofer.

Listed main-plan features:

- Player and world ESP overlays (per partner menu).
- Aimbot modules (per partner menu).

Active subscribers receive compatibility updates after major game changes without an additional update charge according to the supplier.

## Lifetime plan ($150)

Lifetime access is a one-time $150 payment with no monthly renewal.

The supplier states the same ESP and aimbot feature set as monthly access.

Four monthly periods would cost $140; five would cost $175, so lifetime is worth comparing if you expect long-term use.
