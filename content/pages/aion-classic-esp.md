# Aion Classic ESP and Aimbot: Main Plan Features

This page covers the **main plan** modules: **ESP** and **aimbot**.

Cloud DMA and HWID Spoofer are documented on their own routes and are **not** part of the main plan price.

## Player ESP

Player ESP is intended to surface extra player information on screen—nameplates, distance, and related filters described on the partner listing.

Customization details change between builds. Use the current showcase rather than assuming specific box styles or colors.

## World and minimap ESP

The partner groups additional awareness tools—world markers, minimap or radar views, and distance helpers—under ESP on supported builds.

We summarize what the listing names; we do not add features that are absent from the vendor menu.

## Aimbot (main plan)

Aimbot modules ship with the same main plan license as ESP. They are **not** included with Cloud DMA or HWID Spoofer.

Verify targeting options, hotkeys, and safety toggles on the live partner page before purchase.

## Not on the main plan

The following are **supporting products**, billed separately:

- **Cloud DMA** — infrastructure for supported stacks ([guide](/cloud-dma-for-aion-classic/)).
- **HWID Spoofer** — standalone utility checkout when offered by the partner.

Do not assume either product is bundled with $35 monthly or $150 lifetime ESP and aimbot access.
