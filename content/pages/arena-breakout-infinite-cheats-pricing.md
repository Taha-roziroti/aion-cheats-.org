## Cloud DMA price and access

Cloud DMA is listed as a separate product, with the following access option:

| Product | Price | Duration |
|---|---:|---|
| Cloud DMA | $50 | Lifetime |

Cloud DMA is required for full functionality on supported ABI builds.

Its setup uses a single Windows PC with a Hyper-V-based virtual environment. This configuration has its own system requirements and additional separately purchased services.
