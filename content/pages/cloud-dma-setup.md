# Cloud DMA setup guide for Aion Classic

This guide summarizes the published Cloud DMA workflow for Aion Classic. It does not replace vendor install email steps. Use the [Cloud DMA Hyper-V overview](/cloud-dma-for-aion-classic/) for pricing and purchase requirements.

## Before you install

Confirm the items on the [PC requirements list](/aion-classic-system-requirements/) page:

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Hyper-V capable edition
- HVCI, Core Isolation, TPM, and Secure Boot enabled
- Intel VT-x or AMD-V enabled in firmware
- At least 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for the guest)
- Stable broadband internet

Budget separate purchases for GREENWARD, Static Server, and AWS in addition to the listed Cloud DMA license.

## Single-PC Hyper-V flow

The vendor describes a single physical PC running a Hyper-V guest alongside your desktop. You enable virtualization in firmware, turn on the Hyper-V role, and complete activation through the encrypted service link in vendor documentation.

We do not publish click-by-click steps that are not in supplier materials. After checkout, follow the vendor PDF or ticket instructions.

## Budgeting GREENWARD, Static Server, and AWS

The supplier treats Cloud DMA as the infrastructure license only. You still purchase:

- **GREENWARD** for activation routing (fee on partner listing).
- **Static Server** hosting component (separate SKU).
- **AWS** usage billed outside the $50 lifetime license quote we publish.

Add all three lines to your spreadsheet before you review
[main-plan monthly and lifetime pricing](/aion-classic-pricing/)—those ESP and aimbot prices never include Cloud DMA.

## Proof you can verify before checkout

Screenshot your firmware virtualization pages, Windows optional features panel, and Core isolation settings. If
Hyper-V cannot install, do not assume the $50 Cloud DMA license will activate on that PC.

Re-read the Hyper-V overview and PC requirements links at the top of this guide and confirm both checklists pass on your build before you open vendor checkout.
